## Slide 5

The progress of human civilization, in some sense, is a progress of improving the precision of measurement. 

From the unit defined by the lengths of foot, hand, step, etc., to that with vernier calipers, microscope, laser ranging device, etc.

The measurement precision is improved greatly, and the quality of people’s life has greatly improved at the same time.

## Slide 6

Safety and Performance: In industries like aerospace and medical devices, high-level precision ensures equipment functions correctly, preventing, for example, serious medical consequences or structural failures.

Manufacturing Quality & Waste Reduction: Precise measurements are a cornerstone of lean manufacturing, ensuring parts are produced correctly the first time, reducing material waste, and improving efficiency.

Scientific Advancement: Precise, quantifiable data is necessary for validating scientific theories, such as understanding physical processes.

Daily Technology: Modern life relies on it; for example, GPS, cell phones, and computers cannot function properly without precise timing and frequency measurements.

Cost Control & Efficiency: Precise measurements ensure proper resource allocation in construction and engineering, preventing unnecessary labor and material costs.
